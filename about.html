👋 Who We Are

We Test AI Tools So You Don't Have To

We're a small team of developers, writers, and technologists who are genuinely obsessed with AI tools. We spend hundreds of hours testing every product we review — and we tell you the unvarnished truth.

Our Mission

The AI tool market moves at a brutal pace. New products launch weekly, old ones pivot or die, and vendor marketing is almost always misleading. Our mission is simple: cut through the noise and give you honest, actionable recommendations based on real-world testing.

We started Tech & AI Reviews because we were tired of reading reviews that were either:

  • Written by AI (yes, really — tools reviewing tools)
  • Pure vendor press releases dressed up as editorial
  • Based on a 20-minute free trial rather than real use
  • Published purely to rank for keywords with zero actual testing

We do things differently. Every review on this site is based on:

  • Minimum 2 weeks of hands-on use — not just feature walkthroughs
  • Real-world tasks — we use each tool for actual work, not synthetic benchmarks
  • Multiple reviewers — reducing individual bias
  • Updated regularly — AI tools evolve fast, and our reviews do too

How We Test

Our testing process has three phases:

Phase 1: Discovery & Setup (1-2 days)

We go through the official onboarding, documentation, and initial setup of each tool. We pay for our own accounts — no vendor-provided access that could compromise our independence.

Phase 2: Real-World Testing (2-4 weeks)

This is where we actually use the tool. For an AI writing tool, we write real articles. For an AI coding tool, we write real code that ships. For an AI agent, we give it real tasks and evaluate the output. For automation tools, we build real workflows.

We test on identical tasks across comparable tools to ensure fair head-to-head comparisons.

Phase 3: Analysis & Review (1-2 days)

We compile findings, rate each tool across consistent criteria (listed below), and write the review. All ratings are discussed and agreed upon by our editorial team — not left to a single author's opinion.

How We Rate

Every tool is rated on a 5-star scale across these criteria:

Criterion Weight What We Look For
Performance 30% Does it actually do what it claims? Output quality, accuracy, reliability
Ease of Use 20% Learning curve, UI quality, onboarding, documentation
Value for Money 20% Is the price justified by the output? Free tier quality if applicable
Features 15% Breadth and depth of capabilities, integrations
Support & Community 15% Documentation quality, response times, community size

Editorial Standards

No paid placements. We never accept payment to include a product in our reviews, to improve a rating, or to add a product to a comparison. If a company offers us money, we tell you about it.

No affiliate influence on reviews. Our affiliate relationships are disclosed at the bottom of every page. However, we only recommend products we'd recommend regardless of whether they have affiliate links. The financial incentive never changes our editorial judgment.

We update constantly. AI tools change fast. When a tool we reviewed gets a major update that changes our assessment, we update the review and note the update date prominently.

No AI-generated reviews. Every word on this site is written by a human who actually tested the product. We use AI tools in our workflow, but we don't publish AI output as editorial content.
